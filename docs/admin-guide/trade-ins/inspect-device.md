# Inspect and grade a device

After a parcel is [received](receive-parcel.md), inspect the device and set its grade. This decides what we pay.

**The golden rule: prices only go down.** If the device is as good as or better than the customer said, we pay the original quote. If it's worse, we send a lower offer the customer can accept or reject.

## Steps

1. Open the quote and click **Begin Inspection**.
2. If you see a **Late arrival** box, deal with it first (see [Decide on a late arrival](late-arrival.md)).
3. Choose the **Inspection Grade**. The customer's original grade is marked "(original)".
4. If the model or storage is different from the quote, tick **Device model/storage is different from quoted** and pick the actual device.
5. Finish the inspection:

**Same grade, same device.** Click **Complete Inspection**. The trade-in moves to **Inspected** at the original price. Next, [pay the customer](pay-customer.md).

**Different grade or device.** A **Revised Price (NZD)** box appears, with two choices:

- **Confirm at Original Quote**: use this if the device is as good or better, or you decide to pay the original price anyway. The trade-in moves to **Inspected** at the original price.
- **Send Revised Offer**: enter the lower price **in NZD** and click **Send Revised Offer**. Under the box you'll see what the customer will be offered in their own currency, at the exchange rate locked when they got the quote. The customer is emailed the new offer and the trade-in moves to **Revised**. See [Follow up a revised offer](revised-offer.md).

## Before you finish

- Is there a problem with ownership: activation lock, blacklisting, or signs it's stolen? Don't inspect it. [Put it on hold](on-hold.md) instead.
- Should we not buy it at all (for example, it's not what was described or it's a fake)? Use **Return Device** instead (see [Return a device](return-device.md)).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Enter a price below …"** A revised offer must be lower than the original quote. If you don't want to lower it, use **Confirm at Original Quote**.
- **"In AUD this isn't below the original."** After conversion and rounding, the customer's amount comes out the same as the original. Lower the NZD price a little more.
- **The buttons stay greyed out.** Check you've chosen a grade, picked a device (if you ticked the device box), and answered the late arrival question.
