# Receive a parcel

Do this as soon as a trade-in parcel arrives, before you inspect the device.

## Steps

1. Go to **Trade-in Ops** and click **Receive Parcel**.
2. Scan the barcode on the label. You can also type any of these:
   - the tracking number (or at least 8 characters of it)
   - the TI- reference from the note in the box
   - the device's IMEI
   - the customer's name or email
3. Press **Find**. If there's exactly one match, it opens straight away. If there are several, click the right one.
4. Check the device and customer shown match what's in the box.
5. Enter the device's **IMEI** (15 digits). If it has no IMEI, enter the **Serial number** instead.
6. Click **Receive**.

A green message confirms it, and the screen is ready for the next parcel. The trade-in is now **Received**. Next, [inspect the device](inspect-device.md).

## Warnings you might see

- **"Doesn't match the IMEI on the quote."** The customer gave a different IMEI. Make sure this is the right parcel and the right device. If it is, you can still receive it, then note the difference when you inspect.
- **"Arrived after the expected date."** It will be flagged as a **late arrival**. Check the first-scan date in Australia Post tracking now and write it down. You'll need it to [decide on the late arrival](late-arrival.md) when you inspect.

## Parcels for closed partner trade-ins

Sometimes a parcel arrives for a partner trade-in (such as OPPO) that has already ended. It may have expired unposted, been cancelled, or been marked lost in transit. The partner has already been told the trade-in isn't going ahead, so instead of the receive form you'll see **"Don't receive this parcel"**, with the customer's name, address, email and phone.

1. Click **Log parcel to follow up**. The note is filled in for you; add a photo if it helps, then click **Log Parcel**. Keep the device somewhere separate and labelled.
2. Contact the customer. Either:
   - **Start a new Reflow trade-in** for them: [create and accept a quote](create-and-accept.md) at today's price, with their payout details. Then receive the device against the new quote (**Mark Received** on the quote page). Reflow pays them directly; there's no partner refund or bonus.
   - **Post it back** to the address shown.
3. [Resolve the logged parcel](unmatched-parcel.md) with what happened, for example "New trade-in TI-1200" or "Posted back, tracking 33ABC…".

## If nothing matches

The search covers accepted and shipped trade-ins, expired ones that had a label, and cancelled partner trade-ins. Try another detail, like the customer's name or the TI- reference from the note in the box. If nothing matches, click **Log unmatched parcel** (see [Log and resolve an unmatched parcel](unmatched-parcel.md)).

## Receiving from the quote page

If you already have the quote open, you can click **Mark Received** in the Workflow box instead. You'll be asked for the same IMEI or serial number.
