# Put a trade-in on hold

Put a trade-in on hold while you check a concern about the device: who owns it, whether it's blacklisted, or whether it might be stolen or fraudulent. **Payment is blocked while it's on hold.**

The customer sees "Under Review" and isn't told why.

You can put a trade-in on hold once it's **Received** or **Inspected**.

## Put it on hold

1. Open the quote and click **Put On Hold**.
2. Enter the reason, for example "IMEI shows as blacklisted, checking with carrier".
3. Click **Put On Hold**.

The status changes to **On Hold** and your reason is shown in the Workflow box.

No email is sent. Contact the customer if you need information from them.

## When the check is done

| Outcome | What to do |
|---|---|
| All clear | Click **Release Hold**, enter a note (for example "Carrier confirmed not blacklisted") and confirm. The trade-in goes back to where it was (Received or Inspected), and you carry on. |
| We won't buy it, and it can go back | Click **Return Device** and enter the reason (see [Return a device](return-device.md)). |
| Handed to the police or another authority | Click **Cancel Quote**. The only reason allowed is **Surrendered to authorities**. Add a note with the reference number. |
