# Decide on a late arrival

A trade-in is flagged **Late arrival** if the parcel arrives after its **Expected by** date, or after the trade-in has expired. The badge appears next to the status at the top of the quote.

Our terms say a customer must lodge the parcel within 14 days of us sending the label. A late arrival might still have been posted on time and just slowed down by the carrier, so you need to check before you inspect.

## Steps

1. Open the quote and click **Begin Inspection**. The **Late arrival** box shows the post-by date and a link to the tracking.
2. Open **AusPost tracking** and find the **first scan**: the date the parcel was lodged.
3. Choose one:

| Choose | When |
|---|---|
| **Lodged on time (carrier delay)** | The first scan is on or before the post-by date. Treat it like any other trade-in. |
| **Lodged late: honour the quote** | Lodged after the post-by date, but only by a little, or the price hasn't changed much. **This is the usual choice for a late parcel.** |
| **Lodged late: reassess at current pricing** | Lodged well after the post-by date **and** today's price is significantly lower. |

4. Add a note with the first-scan date, for example "First scan 18 Oct".
5. Continue the inspection as normal (see [Inspect and grade a device](inspect-device.md)).

The decision is saved with the trade-in and shown on the Late arrival badge. You only make it once.

## Honour or reassess?

Honour the quote unless the parcel is clearly late **and** the price has dropped significantly. To check today's price, open **Quotes → Create Quote**, pick the device and grade, read the price, then close the dialog without creating.

If you reassess, send a revised offer at today's price (see [Inspect and grade a device](inspect-device.md)). The revised price box only appears if you change the grade or the device. If neither has changed, you can't send a lower offer from the inspection screen, so ask a manager.
