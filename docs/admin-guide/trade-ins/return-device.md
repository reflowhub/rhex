# Return a device

Return a device when we won't buy it, or when the customer rejects a revised offer. Returning is two steps: start the return, then confirm the device has been sent back.

## Start the return

A trade-in moves to **Returning** in one of these ways:

- **You start it**, from **Received**, **Inspected** or **On Hold**:
  1. Open the quote and click **Return Device**.
  2. Enter the reason, for example "Not the device described" or "Activation locked, customer can't remove".
  3. Click **Return Device**.
- **The customer rejects a revised offer**, or doesn't answer it in time. This happens automatically (see [Follow up a revised offer](revised-offer.md)).

For consumer trade-ins, **no email is sent** when a trade-in moves to Returning. If you started the return, tell the customer why and confirm their return address. Partner trade-ins (such as OPPO) email the customer that the trade-in won't go ahead. The address on the quote is in the Customer Details box.

Everything waiting to go back is listed in **Trade-in Ops → To return**, oldest first.

## Send it back

1. Pack the device and post it with Australia Post tracking to the customer's address.
2. On the quote, click **Mark Returned**. The box shows the address to return it to.
3. Enter the **Return tracking number** (optional, but the customer can only track the parcel if you add it) and the label's **Cost (AUD)** (optional, but it helps when tracking label spend).
4. Click **Mark Returned**.

The trade-in moves to **Returned**, which is the end of the journey. The customer is emailed that their device is on its way back, with the tracking number and a tracking link if you entered one.

Only mark it returned once the parcel has been posted, because the email tells the customer it's on its way.

## When the partner makes the return label

Some partners make the return label (set per partner in **Partners → Shipping labels**). In **To return**, those rows say **Waiting for {partner}** until the label arrives.

1. When the partner sends the label, open the quote. In the **Return Label** box, choose the **Label PDF**, enter the **Tracking number** and the **Cost (AUD)** if known, and click **Add Label**.
2. Click **Download PDF**, print the label and post the device with it.
3. Click **Mark Returned**. The box already shows the partner's tracking number, so there's nothing to fill in.

If the partner's label doesn't come, you can still post the device with our own label and mark it returned with our tracking number as usual. We pay for that one.

## Good to know

- You can't return a trade-in that's already **Paid**.
- You can't start a return from **Revised**. The customer rejects the offer, or it expires (see [Follow up a revised offer](revised-offer.md)).
