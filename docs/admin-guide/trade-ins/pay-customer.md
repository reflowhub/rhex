# Pay the customer

Pay once the trade-in is **Inspected**. That happens when you complete the inspection at the original price, or after the customer accepts a revised offer.

## Steps

1. Open the quote and click **Mark Paid**. The **Confirm Payment** box shows:
   - **Amount**: what to pay, in the customer's currency (with the NZD equivalent underneath)
   - **Basis**: "Original quote" or "Revised offer"
   - **Pay to**: the PayID or bank account the customer gave us
2. **Send the payment first**, from the bank, for exactly that amount to exactly those details.
3. Back in admin, click **Mark Paid**.

The trade-in moves to **Paid**, which is the end of the journey. The customer is emailed to confirm the payment. The Workflow box records the amount, where it went, who marked it paid and when.

Mark Paid can't be undone, so only click it once the money has gone.

## Next: add it to stock

Once a trade-in is inspected or paid, the device can be added to inventory: **Inventory → Add Inventory → Receive from Quote**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Payout details are missing."** The customer didn't give complete PayID or bank details. Contact them for the details, then:
  1. On the quote, click **View Customer Profile**.
  2. Click **Edit** and fill in the payment details.
  3. Save. The details are copied onto their open trade-ins.
  4. Go back to the quote and try **Mark Paid** again.
- **Mark Paid isn't there.** The trade-in isn't Inspected yet, or it's **On Hold**. Payment is blocked until the hold is released.
