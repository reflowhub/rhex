# Refund unused labels

Australia Post refunds labels that were never used if we ask within **90 days**. Unused labels end up in **Trade-in Ops → Labels to refund** when:

- the label was **replaced** with a new one
- the trade-in **expired** without being posted (day 44)
- the trade-in was **cancelled** before it was posted

The **Why** column shows which. Work through this queue once a week. The oldest labels are at the top, and labels are flagged **Urgent** from day 75.

## Steps

For each label:

1. Check the tracking number in Australia Post tracking.
2. Then:
   - **No scans:** request the refund in the Australia Post portal, then click **Mark refunded**.
   - **Has been scanned:** the label was used, so it can't be refunded. Click **Was used**.

Either way, the label leaves the queue.

## Good to know

- A **Was used** label on an expired or cancelled trade-in may mean a parcel is on its way to us. Keep an eye out for it, and see [Receive a parcel](receive-parcel.md) or [Log and resolve an unmatched parcel](unmatched-parcel.md) if it arrives.
- Click the reference to open the trade-in if you need more detail.
