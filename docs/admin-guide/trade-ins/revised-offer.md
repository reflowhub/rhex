# Follow up a revised offer

When you send a revised offer during [inspection](inspect-device.md), the trade-in moves to **Revised**. The customer is emailed the new price and has **7 days** to respond on their quote page.

The quote page shows "Waiting for customer/partner response" and the date the offer expires.

## What can happen

| What happens | Result |
|---|---|
| Customer clicks **Accept Revised Offer** | Moves to **Inspected** at the revised price. [Pay the customer](pay-customer.md). |
| Customer clicks **Reject & Return Device** | Moves to **Returning**. [Return the device](return-device.md). |
| No response by the expiry date | Moves to **Returning** automatically within the hour. [Return the device](return-device.md). |

You don't need to do anything while you wait.

## The customer accepts by phone or email

If the customer tells you they accept but can't use their quote page:

1. Open the quote and click **Force Accept (on behalf of customer)**.
2. Enter the reason, for example "Accepted by phone, 12 Oct, spoke to Sam".
3. Click **Accept Revision**.

The trade-in moves to **Inspected** at the revised price. Your reason is saved in the History box.

Only do this when the customer has clearly agreed. You can still do it after the offer's expiry date, but only until the trade-in moves to Returning.

## The customer rejects by phone or email

You can't reject on their behalf. Ask them to click **Reject & Return Device** on their quote page. Otherwise, the offer will be treated as rejected when it expires.

## Changing the response window

The 7 days is set in **Settings → Trade-Ins → Revision Response Window**. A change only affects offers sent after you save it.
