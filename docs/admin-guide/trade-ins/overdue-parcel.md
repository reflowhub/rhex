# Follow up an overdue parcel

A parcel is overdue when it hasn't arrived by the **Expected by** date: the 14-day post-by window plus 10 days for delivery (day 24 after the label was sent).

## Steps

1. Go to **Trade-in Ops → Overdue**. The most overdue parcels are at the top.
2. Copy the tracking number and check it in Australia Post tracking.
3. Act on what tracking shows:

| Tracking shows | Status | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No scans | Accepted | The customer hasn't posted it. Optionally contact them. If nothing happens, the trade-in expires automatically on day 44 and the label goes to the refund queue. |
| No scans | Shipped | The customer said they posted it but nothing has scanned. Contact them and ask for their lodgement receipt. |
| Scanned, still moving | Either | Wait. It'll be flagged as a late arrival when you receive it. |
| Delivered | Either | Look for the parcel here. If you find it, [receive it](receive-parcel.md). |
| Lost, or stuck for weeks | Shipped | Raise it with Australia Post. If it's confirmed lost, [cancel the trade-in](cancel.md) with **Lost in transit**. |

Write down what you found (for example the first-scan date). You'll need it if the parcel arrives late (see [Decide on a late arrival](late-arrival.md)).

## Good to know

- A trade-in leaves this queue once it's received, cancelled or expired.
- Only **Accepted** trade-ins expire automatically. A **Shipped** one stays here until you receive or cancel it.
- An expired trade-in can still be received if the parcel turns up later.
